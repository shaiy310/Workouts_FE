Progressive Overload & Execution Guidelines

  • Rest Interval Management: To stay strictly under the 40-minute limit, strictly track rest periods using a timer. Move immediately between superset and tri-set exercises without stopping.
  • Progressive Overload: Once you hit the top rep count across all sets for an exercise with good form, increase the weight by 1–2 kg in the following session.
  • Tempo & Mind-Muscle Connection: Focus on a controlled 2-second negative (eccentric) phase on all pressing and curling movements to maximize mechanical tension.
